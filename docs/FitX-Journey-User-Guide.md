# FitX Journey — User Guide

Plain-language guide to everything you can do as a member.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. **Create an account** — from the landing page tap *Start Free* / *Join Now*.
   You can sign up with email + password or with Google. The eye icon next to the
   password field lets you check what you typed before submitting.
2. **Onboarding** — a short guided flow asks for:
   - Your goal (lose fat, maintain, build muscle)
   - Age (you must be 16 or older), height, weight, sex
   - Activity level and unit preference (metric or imperial)
   From this we calculate your daily calories and macro split automatically
   (Mifflin-St Jeor formula + activity multiplier + goal adjustment).
3. **Member ID** — every account gets a short code such as `FX-7K2Q4M`.
   You can copy it from *Settings → Profile*. Support and coaches use it to find
   your account, so share it when you open a ticket.

You only do onboarding once. After that, signing in takes you straight to your
dashboard.

---

## 2. Dashboard

Your home screen shows:
- Today's calories, water and workout status at a glance
- Your current streak
- Quick links into workouts, nutrition and progress
- Announcements from the team when there are any

---

## 3. Workouts

**Workout Planner** (`/workout-planner`)
- Build a workout by adding exercises, sets, reps and weight.
- Search the exercise database by name, body part or equipment; each exercise
  includes a demo animation and target muscles.
- **Coach Library tab** — plans and workouts published by the team. Open one and
  copy it into your own planner.
- When you save a workout you can optionally **Sync to Calendar** and pick which
  days it should repeat on. Skip that step if you just want it stored in your
  planner.

**Workout Session** (`/workout-session`)
- Run a saved workout live: tick off sets, log the weight you actually lifted,
  and rest between sets. Finishing a session logs it to your history, feeds your
  progress charts and can create new personal records.

**Calendar** (`/calendar`)
- Month view of scheduled and completed workouts. Tap a day to see or start what
  is planned.

---

## 4. Nutrition

**Food log** (`/nutrition`)
- Your daily ring shows calories consumed vs. your goal, with protein, carbs and
  fat underneath.
- Meals are split into Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks. Pick a meal, search
  the food database, and choose your item.
- **Portion editor** — before saving, change the serving (grams, cups, pieces,
  etc.). Calories and macros recalculate as you adjust. Save and it lands in the
  right meal for that day.
- **Barcode scan** — scan a packaged product with your camera to pull its
  nutrition data straight in.
- **Adjust nutrition goals** — override the calculated targets whenever you want;
  the ring and macros follow your new numbers.
- Everything saves per day to your profile, so switching dates shows that day's
  real log.

**Hydration**
- Tap to add glasses/ml; the animated water level fills as you go. Your daily
  target follows your body metrics.

**Recipes** (`/recipes`) and **Meal Planner** (`/meal-planner`)
- Search recipes with full nutrition breakdowns and save favourites.
- Drop recipes onto days of the week to build a meal plan.
- **Grocery list** (`/grocery`) — generated from your meal plan; tick items off
  as you shop.

---

## 5. Progress and records

**Progress** (`/progress`)
- Weight trend chart, total change since your starting weight, workout volume
  and streaks. Log a new weigh-in any time; your starting weight is seeded once
  from onboarding (it is never duplicated on repeat logins).

**Records** (`/records`) — your trophy room: best lifts, longest streaks and
personal bests with the date each was set.

**Rewards** (`/rewards`) — points, tier ladder and badges earned for logging
workouts, hitting nutrition goals and keeping streaks alive.

---

## 6. Settings

Side-rail sections:
- **Profile** — name, photo, member ID. Your photo and name sync everywhere,
  including what the admin team sees.
- **Body metrics** — height, weight, age, sex. Changing these recalculates your
  calorie and hydration targets.
- **Preferences** — metric/imperial. The choice applies app-wide (kg↔lb, cm↔ft,
  ml↔fl oz) instantly.
- **Notifications** — which reminders and announcements you receive.
- **Appearance** — light or dark theme; saved to your profile so it follows you
  to other devices. The navbar sun/moon icon toggles it instantly.
- **Account** — password/sign-out and account actions.

---

## 7. Support

`/support` — submit a ticket with a category, priority and description. You can
see the status of every ticket you have opened (open → in progress → resolved)
and reply in the thread when the team answers.

---

## 8. Offline and installing

- The app keeps working with a cached copy of your data if you lose connection —
  an offline banner appears, and changes sync once you are back online.
- You can install it to your phone home screen from your browser's *Add to home
  screen* option.

---

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| Stuck on onboarding after sign-up | Finish the goal step — onboarding completes once a goal is saved. |
| Food search returns nothing | Check connection; retry in a few seconds (the food provider rate-limits bursts). |
| Photo upload fails | Use an image under 5 MB (JPG/PNG). |
| Old version showing after an update | Hard-refresh once; the app clears its old cache automatically. |
| Units look wrong | Settings → Preferences → pick metric or imperial. |
